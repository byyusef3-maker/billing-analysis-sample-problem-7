# Utility Billing Analysis (CTA Sample Problem #7)
**Created by: Yusef Berry**

This project is a complete end‑to‑end utility billing analysis covering electricity and natural gas usage across two billing years (2019–2020 and 2020–2021).  
It includes raw data cleaning, rate calculations, annualization, visualization prep, and a final “End Use” comparison chart.

This repository contains:
- Cleaned and aligned billing data  
- Electric and gas rate calculations  
- Annual totals and averages  
- Monthly electric + gas comparison tables  
- Final 12.3 End Use Chart  
- Exported PDF deliverable for submission  

---

## 📊 **Project Overview**
This analysis takes raw utility billing data and transforms it into a structured, analyzable dataset.  
The workflow includes:

### **1. Data Cleaning & Alignment**
- Converted date serials into readable dates  
- Standardized column names  
- Calculated total bill amounts  
- Added billing cycle day counts  
- Computed electric and gas rates per billing period  

### **2. Year Splitting**
Data was separated into:
- **2019–2020 billing year**
- **2020–2021 billing year**

This allows for year‑over‑year comparison of:
- Electricity usage  
- Gas usage  
- Total costs  
- Demand (kW)  
- Rate changes  

### **3. Annual Totals & Averages**
Includes:
- Total kWh and therms  
- Total annual cost  
- Average monthly usage  
- Average electric and gas rates  
- Average demand  

### **4. Visualization Prep**
Created structured tables for:
- Monthly electric comparison  
- Monthly gas comparison  
- Combined “End Use” chart data  

### **5. Final Deliverables**
- **Excel workbook** with all calculations  
- **PDF export** for submission  
- **12.3 End Use Chart** comparing both years  

---

## 📈 **Key Insights**
- Electricity usage increased from 2019–2020 to 2020–2021  
- Gas usage also increased slightly year‑over‑year  
- Electric rates rose, while gas rates decreased  
- Summer months show the highest electric usage due to cooling load  
- Winter months show the highest gas usage due to heating load
